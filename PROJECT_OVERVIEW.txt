1. SYSTEM PURPOSE & PROBLEM CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Manufacturing and industrial plants lose 15% to 40% of their operational capacity 
and incur millions in emergency repair bills due to unexpected machine breakdowns.

Traditional approaches fail:
- Reactive Maintenance ("Run-to-failure"): Machines break unexpectedly, halting lines.
- Calendar Maintenance ("Time-based"): Parts are replaced prematurely every 90 days,
  wasting budget while still missing fast-developing mechanical defects.

The Condition-Based Solution:
This system continuously analyzes vibration velocity (RMS mm/s) and high-frequency 
bearing shock impact (Peak-G) across rotating equipment to forecast Remaining Useful 
Life (RUL) and schedule repairs before catastrophic breakdown occurs.

3. WHAT IS WHAT (MODULE GUIDE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* SIMULATION COCKPIT:
  Floating top-right dark minimal bar. Allows live 1-click injection of real plant 
  scenarios (Vibration Spike 8.6 mm/s, Bearing Defect 5.2g, RUL Drop 14h, Reset Fleet) 
  so reviewers can see dynamic reactivity in seconds without waiting months for machines 
  to degrade.

* FLEET OVERVIEW & METRIC CARDS:
  Aggregated KPI cards: Overall Health (%), Active Alerts (count), Needs Service Soon 
  (<200h RUL count), and Scheduled Repairs.

* LIVE MACHINE VIBRATION WAVE:
  Smooth cubic Bezier spline area chart. Features dynamic auto-zoom scaling, machine 
  dropdown switcher, live pulsing tip beacon, and dashed red warning limit line.
  Automatically switches to any machine experiencing an active alert.

* EQUIPMENT ALERTS:
  Real-time anomaly detection with failure mode descriptions (e.g. Spindle Unbalance, 
  Bearing Flaking, Impeller Cavitation), risk percentage (0-100%), and sensor attribution.
  Equipped with a green 1-click Resolve button to heal equipment.

* REMAINING USEFUL LIFE (RUL):
  Prognostic wear countdown showing estimated operating hours remaining with upper 
  and lower confidence intervals.

* REPAIRS & TASKS:
  Actionable maintenance dispatch with assigned technician crews, scheduled execution 
  dates, downtime estimates, and OEM replacement parts kitting.

5. ANTICIPATED REVIEWER QUESTIONS & SIMPLE ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What is Remaining Useful Life (RUL)?
A: RUL is the projected number of operating hours a machine can safely run before 
   mechanical degradation causes catastrophic failure. It is estimated by tracking 
   vibration velocity against ISO wear curves.

Q: Why is Vibration RMS the primary metric instead of temperature?
A: Vibration is a leading indicator. Unbalance or bearing fatigue creates mechanical 
   vibration days or weeks before friction generates heat. Temperature is a lagging indicator.

Q: What happens under the hood when clicking "Resolve"?
A: The backend validates if any other unresolved alerts remain for that machine. 
   If none exist, it resets the machine's baseline in MongoDB (1.8 mm/s, 96% health), 
   logs a clean telemetry reading, and broadcasts a Socket.io event to update all browsers.
